# ⚡ Institutional Dividend, REIT & InvIT Screener

A quantitative institutional screening and ranking engine for Indian Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs), and High-Yield Dividend Equities listed on the NSE & BSE.

### 🌟 Key Features
- **Institutional Buy Conviction Ranking (Rank #1 = Most Preferred)**: Evaluates yield spread over 10Y Indian Sovereign G-Sec (6.80%), institutional quality score, 52-week high pullback margin of safety, statutory payout sustainability, and technical accumulation zones.
- **Top Conviction Showcase**: Displays top 3 conviction buys with real-time CMP, yield, sovereign spread, and composite buy score.
- **Autonomous Auto-Refresh Daemon**: Continuously synchronizes quotes and technicals every 15 minutes.
